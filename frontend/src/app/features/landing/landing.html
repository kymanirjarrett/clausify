From the client's draft to terms you can accept.

  1. Drop the PDF on your dashboard

    Text-based PDFs up to 10 MB. The text is extracted immediately and the file is discarded.

  2. Each clause is checked against fair terms

    Findings are scored from 0 to 100 across eight categories, with an overall risk level for the contract.

  3. Return corrected wording

    Every clause outside tolerance comes with replacement language to propose in your reply.

The tolerance schedule

Eight categories account for most of the terms that cost freelancers money or future work. Each finding names its category, so you can see where a contract concentrates its risk.
